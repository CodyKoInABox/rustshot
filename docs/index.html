The practical details

Good to know.

Which systems does RustShot support?

RustShot is a 64-bit native application for Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Does it need an installer or an account?

No. RustShot is distributed as a portable ZIP and does not require an account.

Where do my screenshots go?

You choose. Copy to the clipboard, save manually, or configure a local automatic-save folder.

Can I verify the performance claims?

Yes. The benchmark driver, methodology, validation rules, and aggregate report are all public in the repository.
